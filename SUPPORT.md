# Getting help

* **Using the app?** Open **Settings > About & support** in the app: it has the guides, and **Copy support info** gives you a block of facts (version, phone, storage) to paste into a message. It contains nothing about your sales, customers or passwords.
* **Something is broken, or you have an idea:** [open an issue](https://github.com/Baker0o7/MiniMart-Pos/issues/new/choose). Say what you did, what you expected and what happened. Blur customer names, phone numbers and M-Pesa codes in screenshots.
* **A security problem:** please do **not** open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
* **Want to help build it?** See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [roadmap](docs/ROADMAP.md).
